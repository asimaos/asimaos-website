Everything planned for %os% before its first release is now in the next test version, apart from
what would cost money. It is being built and tested in virtual machines now, then on real PCs.

## %appcenter%

One app for software of every kind that runs on Linux: Flatpak apps from Flathub, snaps, AppImages,
Android apps (with Waydroid and F-Droid), Windows programs (in Bottles, with Wine), and developer
tools from Nix, Homebrew, PyPI, npm, crates.io, Go and Conda. Purposes run along the top, and each
kind of app has its own row. Opening an app file shows what it is before anything is installed, and
updates of every kind are one click. KDE's Discover stays for those who like it.

## Games

- **Game mode in gamescope**, with Steam's full-screen interface, as on SteamOS.
- **A handheld's own buttons and gyro**, through InputPlumber.
- **Controllers of every kind**: XInput and DirectInput, wired, over Bluetooth, through wireless
  receivers, and Microsoft's Xbox Wireless Adapter, whose firmware %gaming% downloads from
  Microsoft when you ask it to.

## Safety and repairs

- **Repair an installed %os%** from the USB stick, with screens: put the start-up back, install
  the stick's version, or bring the settings and accounts back from a snapshot. Your files are never
  touched.
- **Updates skip versions**: any version from the past year updates straight to the newest one.
- **%shield% keeps a zone for each network**: Public, Home, Developer, or Not secure for a network
  you trust completely.
- **Signed system extensions only**, and TPM unlock that keeps working when the firmware's Secure
  Boot lists are updated.

## And more

The brightness of every screen, external monitors included; disk limits for containers and virtual
machines; Flatpak repair; and the two versions on the disk sharing what they have in common, so
they take less room.

The first public release comes when the tests on real PCs say it's ready. Follow along here, or
with the [RSS feed](/news/rss.xml).
